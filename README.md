# Orchid Tour & Travels

Orchid Tour & Travels offers curated hospitality experiences across North Bengal's rich landscapes, connecting travelers with nature through distinct homestay properties. This repository hosts the central landing page that bridges two unique regional retreats:

* **Orchid Hill View Homestay (Sittong II):** Situated in the heart of the Shelpu-Sittong belt, famous for its orange orchards and panoramic Himalayan views. This property provides an authentic eco-tourism experience focused on village walk tours, mountain serenity, and local tea garden heritage.
* **Green Gold Homestay (Lataguri):** Located on the doorstep of the Dooars plains and Gorumara National Park. This retreat serves as an ideal base for wildlife enthusiasts, tropical forest safaris, and experiencing the biodiversity of the North Bengal plains.

Through this portal, visitors can explore room amenities, local attractions, photo galleries, and booking details for both properties.
